SAFE Reports Member Support for BECU Merger; Final Approval Still Pending

November 11, 2026

By CreditUnionAI News Staff

Credit Union MergersMember Service

SAFE and BECU reported strong member support on November 10. California’s final approval is still pending, and members can bank as usual.

SAFE Credit Union and BECU reported strong support from SAFE members for their proposed merger in a joint November 10 update. The voting period ended October 27. The update did not give a vote count and said final approval from California’s Department of Financial Protection and Innovation was still pending. The institutions told members to continue banking as usual.

Approval to proceed is not approval to close

In August, BECU announced that the NCUA and state regulators in Washington and California had cleared the proposed combination to advance. That cleared the way for member voting. The latest update distinguishes that earlier clearance from the final California approval the institutions are still waiting for.

The joint FAQ lists January 1, 2027 as the anticipated closing date. That is a target, not evidence that the legal combination or any conversion of member-facing systems has happened. The institutions did not announce a new action for members in their November 10 update.

Give the contact center a date it can stand behind

For credit unions planning a merger, the distinction matters when a member asks which app, card or branch to use. Keep three separate entries in the staff briefing: what the member vote established, when the transaction can legally close, and which services change on which date. Give frontline teams the latest confirmed instructions and a named escalation point for any question the public FAQ does not answer.

After approval, update those instructions service by service. Legal close alone does not establish that logins, scheduled transfers or branch procedures have changed. The recent GECU acquisition illustrates the distinction: the transaction closed, but customer services remained separate.
