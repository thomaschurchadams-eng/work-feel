Canada Names Open-Banking Standards and Complaints Bodies

November 22, 2026

By CreditUnionAI News Staff

Member Service

FDX and OBSI receive distinct roles as Canada prepares its framework. The designations do not establish a nationwide service launch.

Canada designated Financial Data Exchange as its consumer-driven banking technical standards body and the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments as its external complaints body on November 20. The assignments separate the rules for data exchange from the route for unresolved consumer complaints.

The Finance Department announcement says FDX has established a Canada Council with decision-making independence from its broader board. OBSI will investigate participating entities’ unresolved consumer-driven banking complaints and recommend resolutions. The department describes the designations as implementation preparation; final regulations will specify a staggered coming-into-force approach.

Data access and complaint ownership are different decisions

For U.S. credit-union member-service leaders, this is a comparison, not a new U.S. requirement. A technical connection can move data while leaving the member unsure who owns a problem. An exchange standard and an escalation route address different parts of that experience.

A credit union assessing an account-data partner could ask how an incorrect record moves from the first service contact to the party able to investigate it. The useful evidence would identify the responsible organization and what information follows the complaint—not simply show that an API connection works. That is an editorial operating implication, not a claim that the Canadian system has already demonstrated that result.

The announcement does not prove that every Canadian institution or data provider is participating, that a particular credit union has launched a connection, or that the new framework has eliminated screen scraping. It also does not transfer Canada’s supervisory structure to the United States.

The next material evidence is implementation: finalized commencement details, participation and the functioning consumer journey. Until those are demonstrated, standards designation, complaints designation and live availability should remain separate statements in a board paper or member explanation.
