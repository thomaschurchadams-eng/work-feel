AKUVO Connects Its Collections Platform to the CU*Answers CBX Core

November 23, 2026

By CreditUnionAI News Staff

Lending and Collections

AKUVO describes both daily data exchange and real-time connectivity. For a collections team, the useful question is when a newly posted payment appears in the collector’s queue.

AKUVO has announced a standard connection between its collections platform and CBX, the core processing system from CU*Answers. For a credit union using both systems, the practical question is whether the person about to call a member sees the same payment information as the person who took the payment.

In its November 23 announcement, AKUVO says the connection can exchange daily account information and supports both batch and real-time connectivity. It does not say which account fields update immediately at any given credit union, or identify a live institution where all balances and payment statuses already do so.

What the batch listing actually says

CU*Answers describes a daily collections file for AKUVO in its service catalog. That specific offering is marked “Coming Soon.” The listing helps explain what a batch option would do; it is not confirmation that a credit union can order or turn on that service today. A lender evaluating the integration should ask which feed is available for its installation, what the file contains and when changes reach the collections screen.

Follow one payment through both systems

Consider a test account with a payment posted shortly before a collection call is due. A lending team could record when CBX shows the payment, when AKUVO shows it and whether the account still appears in the calling queue. If a collector would see an obsolete amount, the team has a specific decision to make: refresh the data, verify the balance in the core or hold the call. This is a proposed acceptance test, not a reported customer result.

The announcement did not report collections recoveries, avoided calls or implementation times at specific credit unions. Those are separate measures from connecting the systems.
