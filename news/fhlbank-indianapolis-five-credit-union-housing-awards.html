Five Credit Unions Named in $3.6 Million of FHLBank Housing Awards

November 21, 2026

By CreditUnionAI News Staff

Operations

The 2026 award list links five credit unions to projects with 152 AHP-assisted units. Those are subsidy awards—not proof that homes or services are complete.

Five credit unions appear as member institutions beside $3.6 million in subsidies in FHLBank Indianapolis's 2026 Affordable Housing Program award list. The associated projects total 152 AHP-assisted units.

These entries are not the program total.

The five credit-union entries

  • MidWestAmerica Federal Credit Union: 144 W. Oak Street in Butler, Indiana—$1 million and 10 AHP-assisted units.
  • TBA Credit Union: Court Street Station in Kalkaska, Michigan—$600,000 and six assisted units within an eight-unit project.
  • Notre Dame Federal Credit Union: New Day Intake Center in South Bend, Indiana—$1 million and 126 assisted units.
  • orsa credit union: Orchard Pines I in Hillman, Michigan—$500,000 and five assisted units.
  • Advia Credit Union: Simmons Avenue Housing in Battle Creek, Michigan—$500,000 and five assisted units.

The assisted-unit measure must be read in the context of each project. In particular, the intake-center entry should not be converted into a claim about 126 newly built houses or households. The five projects list 154 total units, which differs from their 152 assisted units.

Award and completion are different records

For credit union operations leaders, the useful next distinction is the handoff from an award to documented project activity. A list naming a member institution does not establish that the subsidy has been disbursed, that construction is complete or that residents are already being served.

FHLBank's program resources include disbursement and monitoring material. That makes the operational question specific: which party owns each next record, and what evidence supports the milestone being reported?

For external reporting, retain the project, sponsor and member-institution identities alongside the amount and the measure. Do not substitute the total project size for assisted units or attribute the subsidy amount to a credit union's own capital. Those shortcuts turn a useful regional award into a misleading output claim.

The 2026 list is a dated award record. It supports recognizing these participating credit unions and the projects associated with them; later disbursement and completion claims need their own evidence. The exact original public posting time was not independently established for this brief.
