5Point Selects Digital Self-Service; Launch and Service Results Remain Unreported

November 20, 2026

By CreditUnionAI News Staff

Member Service

Mahalo says 5Point chose its digital banking platform. Planned member controls are specific; a live rollout and measured service gains are not established.

5Point Credit Union has selected Mahalo Banking for its next digital banking platform, according to a supplier announcement dated November 19. The Texas credit union is planning additional member self-service and business banking capabilities, with connectivity to Corelation KeyStone.

Selection-announcement brief: The release names beneficiary management, stop payments, certificate renewals and card controls among the intended functions. It includes comments from 5Point's senior vice president of information technology, Marla Rucker. It does not give a live-launch date or measured service improvements.

A feature list is not a completed member task

For member-service leaders, the useful distinction is between making a control visible and enabling a member to finish the underlying request. A button can be available while an exception still needs employee attention. That is an operating question for a rollout review, not evidence of a defect in either institution or supplier.

Consider a hypothetical member who starts a certificate-renewal request but encounters a product restriction. The relevant result would include whether the request completed, what explanation the member received and whether an employee could pick up the same context. Counting visits to the renewal screen would not answer those questions.

Similarly, a change to a beneficiary record should be evaluated against the institution's actual authorization process and confirmation evidence. The existence of a digital option does not by itself establish that every account type or member situation is supported.

The announcement presents less manual work and more capacity for complex needs as anticipated benefits. This brief has not tested the platform, its integration or those outcomes. A source-backed selection is meaningful procurement news; it is not a claim that the transition has finished.

A practical next comparison is therefore task-specific: which requests members can complete, which exceptions remain assisted and what evidence confirms the final result. Keep that distinction visible when turning a platform decision into a service-improvement forecast.
