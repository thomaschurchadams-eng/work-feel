TruStage Sets Conditional Partner Notice Ahead of Individual Notifications

November 21, 2026

By CreditUnionAI News Staff

Vendor Management

TruStage says its data investigation remains unfinished. Partners would receive 21 calendar days before any required notices go to affected individuals.

TruStage says credit union partners will receive 21 calendar days before any required notifications are sent to impacted individuals, according to its November 19 partner-update transcript. Its investigation into data potentially affected by the cyberattack remains unfinished.

The company said partners would be told the findings relevant to them whether information was impacted or not. If notifications are required, TruStage said it would handle and pay for those notices and a protection package. This is a conditional notification plan, not confirmation that a particular credit union's members were affected.

The next handoff is about findings, not restored service

For vendor-management leaders, this changes the preparation question. October's service-restoration updates concerned whether dependent transactions and requests could resume. A future findings notice would create a separate handoff: which information relates to the institution, who receives it and which local responsibilities follow from it.

A practical first step is to identify the credit union's vendor liaison and the internal owner who can route findings to privacy, legal and member-service colleagues. That coordination can be arranged without assuming there is an affected population or drafting a claim that the investigation has already established harm.

The distinction matters if members contact a credit union before its staff have the relevant findings. Staff need a way to separate a question from a confirmed case, identify the current authoritative vendor communication and escalate requests without speculating about the person's data. This is preparation for a possible notice, not evidence that such inquiries have occurred.

The 21-day interval is TruStage's stated partner lead time. It should not be read as a newly enacted legal deadline or a substitute for an institution's own applicable obligations. The transcript describes planned assistance with regulatory reporting, but this brief has not independently verified that any institution's reporting requirements have been satisfied.

TruStage also estimated on November 19 that completing the data investigation remained at least six to eight weeks away. That estimate is not a guaranteed completion date. Keep the notification workstream separate from operational restoration until actual findings and the institution's responsibilities are established.
