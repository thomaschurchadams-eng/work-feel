Budgeting for Medicare? The 2027 Part B premium is $209.40

Lead source: November 17, 2026

2027 rates and annual-enrollment changes take effect: January 1, 2027

Annual enrollment request deadline: December 7, 2026

The standard monthly premium rises $6.50 in January. December 7 is the deadline for most Medicare open-enrollment plan changes.

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services announced on November 17 that the standard Medicare Part B premium will be $209.40 a month in 2027, up from $202.90 in 2026. The annual Part B deductible rises to $292 from $283. The standard premium isn’t what every person pays: some pay an income-related adjustment, and assistance may help eligible people with costs.

Medicare’s annual open enrollment runs through December 7. The plan must receive an enrollment request by that date; changes made during that window take effect January 1, 2027. The standard Part B amount is separate from any premium a Medicare Advantage or drug plan charges. Changing plans is not required simply because the standard amount increased.

What you can do

Before deciding whether to keep or change coverage, compare your plan’s 2027 premiums, other out-of-pocket costs, drug coverage and participating providers. If you’re considering a change, confirm timing with Medicare or the plan.
