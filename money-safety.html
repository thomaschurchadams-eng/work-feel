Guide 01 · Voice-clone fake emergencies

If a loved one sounds panicked, don’t trust the voice alone.

A scammer may use a short audio clip to imitate a family member, then pressure you to send money immediately. The FTC says to slow down and verify independently.

UrgencyThey insist you must act now.
SecrecyThey tell you not to call anyone else.
Unusual paymentThey ask for a wire, crypto, gift card, or payment app transfer.

What to do next

01

Stop

Do not send money, share a code, or stay on the call because someone creates pressure. Hanging up is okay.

02

Verify

Call or message your loved one using a number you already know—not a number the caller gives you. Check with another trusted person if needed.

03

Secure

If you sent money or shared account information, contact your financial institution right away and report the scam to the FTC.
