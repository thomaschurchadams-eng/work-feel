# CUAI Output Cadence — Production Commitments

## Purpose
This file defines the outward-facing work CreditUnionAI News should produce. It complements the goal hierarchy and role policies; it does not weaken editorial, sourcing, approval, legal, social, or production guardrails.

## Weekday production SLA

### 1. Full editorial output
- **Target:** one qualified full News or Insights article every weekday through the existing Daily Publisher cycle.
- A valid non-publication outcome is acceptable when no candidate clears the editorial/source gates. Quality gates are never lowered to hit volume.
- Every cycle must still leave a machine-observable outcome and handoff.

### 2. Distribution output
- For each published article, make an explicit LinkedIn decision under the existing policy: `posted | scheduled | not selected | blocked`.
- Do not create social volume merely to satisfy cadence. Preserve daily/weekly caps and approved company destination.

### 3. Freshness / alert output
- The weekday CUAI Operating System owns bounded Alert and homepage-freshness work after the article cycle.
- Publish/update Alerts only when qualified material exists under the existing operating policy; do not manufacture alerts.

## Weekly production SLA
By the Friday operating brief, CUAI should be able to account for:
- every weekday article-cycle outcome;
- all material LinkedIn distribution decisions;
- qualified Alert/homepage-freshness changes;
- at least one concrete audience-growth or conversion action tied to G1/G2/G5 when evidence supports it;
- commercial-output progress toward G4, such as a sponsor package, inventory definition, approved prospecting asset, proposal draft, campaign measurement design, or verified revenue event.

Commercial work that requires external outreach, pricing commitment, spend, contract, or other restricted authority remains subject to Tom approval.

## Output quality rule
CUAI is judged on useful qualified output, not raw content count. A week with fewer publications because no story cleared the gates can be healthy if the reason is explicit and the system used the time for qualified growth, commercial, measurement, or reliability work. Repeated failure to produce qualified output must be treated as a constraint to diagnose, not solved by lowering standards.

## Reporting
The CEO should include a concise weekly `Output SLA` status: `met | partially met | missed`, with evidence and the single most important corrective action when not met.
