# CUAI Sponsorship Package — Internal v1

**Status:** Internal commercial definition only. No pricing, outreach, placement, commitment, or external publication is authorized by this file.  
**Goal:** G4 — build a credible CUAI revenue engine while preserving the editorial/commercial firewall.  
**As of:** 2026-09-01

## Evidence and constraints

- The live CUAI Sponsorships & Partnerships page already defines the intended buyer set as organizations serving credit unions in AI-enabled fraud prevention, digital banking, lending technology, data analytics, member experience, and operational automation.
- The existing public policy says sponsorships must be clearly labeled and must preserve editorial independence.
- CUAI currently has a functioning visibly labeled **Sponsored** house-promotion surface, demonstrating that commercial disclosure can be separated from independent article selection.
- The August CFO close is provisional/partial. Complete CUAI operating cost, fully loaded shared-platform cost, sponsor pipeline, and break-even economics are not yet verified. Numeric sponsor pricing would therefore be false precision today.
- Current audience scale is still small. The commercial proposition should be niche relevance, trust, and executive/functional fit—not mass reach or CPM volume claims.

## First package hypothesis: Founding AI Intelligence Partner

### Buyer profile

Credit-union-focused technology providers with a credible AI/technology operating proposition and a need to reach executives or functional leaders in fraud/security, lending, digital banking, payments, data, member service, governance, or operations.

### Value proposition

A limited, clearly disclosed association with a focused credit-union AI intelligence publication, designed for vendors that value qualified industry relevance and credibility more than raw impression volume.

### Candidate pilot inventory

1. **Clearly labeled site partner placement** — a fixed-duration sponsored module separated visually and operationally from independent article rankings, News selection, Alerts, and editorial recommendations.
2. **Partner profile on the Sponsorships surface** — clearly labeled, factual company description and destination link; no implication of editorial endorsement.
3. **Optional sponsored briefing/research support** — only when a separately qualified briefing, event, or research product exists and the sponsorship can be disclosed without controlling independent findings. This is not promised in the initial sale.

The minimum viable pilot should use only inventory that is already technically supportable and separately validated before sale. No sponsor receives influence over article selection, classification, sourcing, conclusions, corrections, or LinkedIn editorial promotion.

## Delivery and measurement

- Use a sponsor-specific tagged destination URL with standard UTM fields and a unique campaign/content identifier.
- Report only privacy-safe aggregate evidence that the instrumentation can actually support, such as placement-page views and attributable outbound clicks. Do not promise individual identities, lead lists, or impression precision that CUAI cannot verify.
- Record commercial placement performance separately from independent editorial performance.
- If sponsor-specific impression/click instrumentation is not validated before a proposal is approved, disclose that limitation rather than estimating.

## Pricing assumption

**Working model only; not approved pricing:** use a fixed-fee pilot rather than CPM pricing because CUAI is selling scarce niche relevance and an explicitly limited partnership, not commodity display inventory. The numeric price remains **TBD** until the CFO completes enough direct/shared cost allocation to support a credible floor and Tom approves the external offer.

## Commercial guardrails

- Every paid placement must be visibly labeled Sponsored, Partner, or equivalent.
- No pay-for-coverage, pay-for-ranking, undisclosed native placement, guaranteed positive treatment, or sponsor veto over independent reporting.
- No external outreach, proposal, pricing commitment, invoice, contract, or placement goes out without Tom approval under current authority boundaries.
- Audience and performance claims must come from current verified analytics; do not use speculative traffic estimates.

After those are resolved, prepare at most one evidence-backed sponsor proposal for Tom approval.
